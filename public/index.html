How it works

From form to arrival

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Complete the form and upload a photo and your passport bio page. You can save it and finish later.

  2. 2

    Pay the fee

    Pay by credit or debit card or a payment wallet. The fee depends on your nationality and visa type.

  3. 3

    Receive your ETA

    Your Electronic Travel Authorisation arrives by email. If a document needs fixing, you'll be asked to re-upload it.

  4. 4

    Fly to India

    Show your printed ETA at an authorised immigration checkpost, where the e-Visa is stamped in your passport.

There are no express or emergency fees

The Government of India charges only the country-specific e-Visa fee, plus bank charges. Websites or agents that promise a faster grant for extra money are not official. Read the Bureau of Immigration's guidance at boi.gov.in.
